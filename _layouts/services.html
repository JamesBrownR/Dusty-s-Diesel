What We Offer

Performance & Dyno Tuning

We don't tune blindly. Every build gets validated on our in-house Dynojet so you leave with real horsepower and torque numbers, not guesses. Whether you want more towing power, better fuel economy, or want to win at the track, we build tunes around your specific truck and your specific goals.

Custom ECU Tuning
Tunes built for your truck, your mods, and your goals. We load, drive, and refine until it is right.
Dynojet Testing
Our in-house Dynojet gives you exact power output measurements and real-world data to optimize your tune.
Performance Upgrades
We install turbos, injectors, fuel systems, and supporting mods to make sure your truck handles the extra power.
